This document serves as the complete, definitive taxonomy of the **Cyber-Táltos Servitor Pantheon**. It bridges Chaos Magic paradigm-shifting, self-management engineering (`time.org`), skill-taxonomy tracking, and archetypal psychology. 

Each servitor is a specialized psychological construct assigned to govern a specific domain, pillar, or transversal current. Every entry details the operational profile, properties, supporting servitors, visual sigil motif, and a detailed 1–2 paragraph historical/fictional background of the character or person from whom the name and energy are derived.

### Taxonomy Hierarchy Key:

- **Tier 0 Exec**: Overarching system overseer and resource coordinator.
- **Tier 1 Category Archons**: Overarching domain patriarchs and category managers.
- **Tier 2 Task Servitors**: Specialized operational units bound to specific active pillars and skills.
- **Transversal Guardians**: Horizontal currents that cut across multiple categories (security, travel, strategy, home, culture, shadow, piloting).
